# Cohvia — Acceptable Use Policy

**Last updated: May 31, 2026**

This Acceptable Use Policy ("**AUP**") describes how the Cohvia Service may and may not be used. It is incorporated into the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) and applies to **SACS Ecommerce Stores Inc.** (operating as "Cohvia") and to every customer, user, and visitor of the Service ("**you**").

We keep this AUP short and plain so you know where the lines are. Violations may result in suspension or termination of your account.

---

## 1. What Cohvia is for

Cohvia is a B2B customer-success platform. You may use the Service to manage your customer relationships, generate insights from data you are entitled to process, and run the customer-success workflows the Service supports. You are responsible for everything that happens under your account, including the actions of every user you invite.

---

## 2. Don't break the law

You must not use the Service:

(a) in violation of any law that applies to you or to the people whose data you submit;

(b) to infringe anyone's intellectual property rights, publicity rights, or privacy rights;

(c) to harass, defame, threaten, or harm anyone;

(d) to engage in fraud, deception, or manipulation; or

(e) to violate export-control, sanctions, or anti-money-laundering laws.

---

## 3. Don't submit data you shouldn't

You must only submit data you have the right and a lawful basis to submit, including any personal data of third parties (for example, individuals named in transcripts or emails). In particular, you must not submit:

(a) **special-category or sensitive personal data** as defined under GDPR, UK GDPR, the CCPA, Quebec Law 25, or similar laws — including health, biometric, genetic, racial or ethnic, religious, political, sexual orientation, or trade-union data — unless we have agreed in writing in advance;

(b) **protected health information** subject to HIPAA or similar health-privacy regimes — Cohvia is not currently configured as a HIPAA business associate;

(c) **financial-account data** such as full payment card numbers, bank account numbers, or government-issued ID numbers (other than the limited billing data needed to pay for the Service, which is handled by our payment provider);

(d) **data of children** under the age of 16 (or higher where local law requires);

(e) data of individuals where you do not have authority, consent, or another lawful basis to submit it for AI processing; or

(f) data subject to a confidentiality obligation that would prohibit its disclosure to Cohvia or our subprocessors.

---

## 4. Don't use AI outputs for high-stakes individual decisions

Cohvia is designed to produce account-level insights for business teams. It is **not** a tool for evaluating, scoring, ranking, or making decisions about identified individuals. You must not use Cohvia Outputs as the sole basis — or a primary basis without meaningful human review — for any decision that produces legal or similarly significant effects on a person, including:

(a) employment decisions (hiring, firing, promotion, demotion, pay, discipline, performance reviews);

(b) access to credit, insurance, housing, education, or essential services;

(c) law-enforcement, immigration, or other government decisions about individuals; or

(d) any decision that would, in the EU AI Act sense, classify the use as "high-risk."

Outputs may be inaccurate, incomplete, or out of date. You are responsible for verifying them before you act on them.

---

## 5. Don't interfere with the Service

You must not:

(a) reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble, or attempt to extract source code, model weights, prompts, or training data, except as permitted by mandatory law;

(b) probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of the Service, or breach security or authentication measures, except under a written security-research agreement with us;

(c) introduce malware, ransomware, or any malicious code;

(d) circumvent usage limits, rate limits, or access controls;

(e) use bots, scrapers, or automated tools to access or extract data in a way that strains the Service or extracts material amounts of data outside the API; or

(f) interfere with the integrity, performance, or availability of the Service for other customers.

---

## 6. Don't misuse the AI features

You must not:

(a) use the Service to generate content that is illegal, defamatory, infringing, or deceptive;

(b) attempt to make the AI produce harmful, hateful, sexually explicit, or violent content;

(c) submit prompts or content designed to bypass safety measures or extract training data from underlying models;

(d) impersonate Cohvia or another person using AI-generated content;

(e) generate or distribute synthetic media (audio, video, images) of real people without their consent and without clear AI-labelling; or

(f) hold Outputs out as human-authored where doing so would mislead someone in a meaningful way.

---

## 7. Don't resell, benchmark, or compete using the Service

You must not:

(a) resell, sublicense, lease, or otherwise make the Service available to anyone outside your own organization's authorized users;

(b) use the Service to build a competing product or service;

(c) use the Service to benchmark performance, accuracy, or pricing for publication or to share with competitors, without our prior written consent; or

(d) copy or imitate the Service, its features, or its user interface.

---

## 8. Cooperation, suspension, and reporting

8.1 **Cooperation.** If we ask you to stop a specific use that we believe violates this AUP or applicable law, you will stop promptly and work with us in good faith to resolve it.

8.2 **Suspension and termination.** We may suspend or terminate access (in whole or in part, with or without notice depending on the severity) if we reasonably believe you have violated this AUP, are creating a security or legal risk, or are causing harm to others. We will use reasonable efforts to limit the scope and duration of suspension and to give notice where appropriate.

8.3 **Reporting abuse.** If you become aware of any violation of this AUP by another user, or of any vulnerability, abuse, or misuse involving the Service, please report it to **privacy@cohvia.com** (or security issues to **security@cohvia.com**). We will not retaliate against good-faith reports.

---

We may update this AUP from time to time. We will post the updated version with a new "Last updated" date and, for material changes, give notice through the Service or by email.
