# Subprocessors

**Last updated: May 31, 2026**

This page lists the third-party subprocessors that **SACS Ecommerce Stores Inc.** (operating as "Cohvia") engages to help provide the Cohvia platform (the "**Service**"). It is incorporated by reference into our [Data Processing Agreement](/legal/dpa) (the "**DPA**") and supports our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

A **subprocessor** is a third party we authorize to process Customer Data on our behalf in order to deliver the Service. Each subprocessor is bound by a written agreement that limits its use of Customer Data to providing services to us and requires data-protection obligations no less protective than those in our DPA.

---

## 1. How we manage subprocessors

- We perform diligence on each subprocessor's security and privacy practices before engaging it.
- We sign a data processing agreement (with EU/UK Standard Contractual Clauses where required) with each subprocessor that processes personal data.
- We route AI processing only to providers that are contractually committed **not to train on submitted data** and, where available, to operate under zero-data-retention terms.
- We remain responsible to you for each subprocessor's performance.

---

## 2. Notice of changes

As described in Section 6.2 of the [DPA](/legal/dpa), we give customers at least **30 days' notice** before adding or replacing a subprocessor that processes Customer Data. Customers may object on reasonable data-protection grounds within the notice period, as set out in the DPA. To receive these notifications, contact **privacy@cohvia.com**.

## 4. Payment processing (independent controller, not a subprocessor)

Payments are handled by **Dodo Payments**, which acts as **merchant of record** and as an **independent controller** of the payment and buyer data it collects directly from customers. Because Dodo determines its own purposes and means for that payment data, it is **not** a Cohvia subprocessor, and its handling of that data is governed by **Dodo's own privacy policy and terms**, not our DPA. We reference it here for transparency.
